    About Me

    Master of Health Administration Johns Hopkins University May 2028
    BA, Public Health & Science Communication Vanderbilt University May 2026

    I’m a journalist focusing primarily on feature writing, with experience in news and science writing as well. My current work sits at the intersection of women’s sports, health, and culture, where I’m interested in the systems that shape athletes’ experiences, the people building women’s sports, and the stories that extend beyond the box score.

    I’m drawn to people: what motivates them, what they’ve overcome, what they care about, and how their experiences connect to something bigger. I also have a special place in my heart for basketball and ultimate frisbee, where my work spans game previews, recaps, analysis, player profiles, and other game-day coverage.

    Whether I’m reporting on healthcare, science, business, or sports, I love asking good questions, listening closely, and finding the human story within the subject. I’m currently available for freelance assignments in healthcare, business, science, and sports.
